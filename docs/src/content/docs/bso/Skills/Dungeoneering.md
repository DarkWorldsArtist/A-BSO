## Starting Out

There are 10 floors in Dungeoneering. Higher floors require higher Dungeoneering levels as well as combat and non-combat skill stats.

Commands:
- Start training: [[/dg start]]
- View floors & requirements: [[/dg floors]]
- View stats & ring progress: [[/dg stats]]
- Buy rewards: [[/dg buy item\:]]

From Floor 6+, you can get tradeable **Gorajan shards**. Combine 2 shards with a Nex armour piece to make **Gorajan armour** using smithing. (Floors 9 and 10 have chances to roll double shards!)

In Floor 10, there's a 1/8,000 per-minute chance to get a **Gorajan bonecrusher (u)**.

You also have a chance on every floor completion to encounter **Alyra**, the Dungeoneering moth pet (rate scales from 1/9,400 on Floor 1 to 1/4,000 on Floor 10).

You receive Dungeoneering tokens at a rate of: **XP × 0.1** (Floor 8 receives +15% tokens, Floor 9 receives +25%, and Floor 10 receives +40%).

## Ring of Kinship

Completing Floor 1 unlocks the **Ring of kinship (tier 1)**. The ring grants a speed boost to Dungeoneering trips.

The Ring of Kinship is upgraded using [[/create item\:Ring of kinship (tier X)]] (the ring must be unequipped in your bank). It does not upgrade automatically by doing trips.

| Tier | Item Name | DG Speed Boost | Requirements to Create |
| ---- | --------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1    | Ring of kinship (tier 1) | +5% | Unlocked on Floor 1 completion (or 1,000 tokens from `/dg buy`) |
| 2    | Ring of kinship (tier 2) | +6% | 50 floor completions (min. 10x on Floors 1–4), 50,000 tokens, 50 Dungeoneering |
| 3    | Ring of kinship (tier 3) | +7% | 150 floor completions (min. 20x on Floors 1–6), 100,000 tokens, 80 Dungeoneering |
| 4    | Ring of kinship (tier 4) | +8% | 300 floor completions (min. 30x on Floors 1–8), 200,000 tokens, 100 Dungeoneering |
| 5    | Overcharged ring of kinship | +10% (+5% DG XP & tokens) | 500 floor completions (min. 40x on Floors 1–10), 118 Dungeoneering, Kinship overcharger (500k tokens from `/dg buy`) |

You can check your current completed floors and upgrade progress at any time using [[/dg stats]]. Progress is also shown at the end of each Dungeoneering trip.

### Gorajan Shard Drop Boosts

- Ring of luck: +5%
- Alyra: +10%
- DG XP Scaling: Scales exponentially from +3% at Level 99 (13m XP) up to +30% at 500m XP (+7% at 110, +13% at 120, +18% at 200m, +24% at 350m)

## Methods

Use XP lamps to reach level 100, then train on the best floor you can with the largest party possible.
